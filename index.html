Genshin Resin Minmaxer

Three calculators for the one question: what's worth banking before the next banner, and what's going in the bin while you wait for it.

Your stockpile

Resin regenerates 1 per 8 minutes — 180 a day. Trounce Domains cost 30 resin for your first 3 clears each week, 60 after.

Resin
