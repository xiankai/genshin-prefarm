Genshin Resin Minmaxer

What is this?
A tool to minmax your resin so it's ready when it counts.
Why hoard?
New character banners introduce a new boss to farm. Stygian Onslaught gives bonus artifacts for its first 10 days.
What does it show?
  • Condensed resin — when to stop spending and start crafting
  • The exact times to act before your resin bar caps out
  • What's left once you've paid for dailies and weekly bosses along the way
  • Transient Resin — a timeline of every copy you can grab and how long each one stays valid
  • Companionship EXP — full by the time a new character lands, ready to pour into them

Act before the bar caps

Your resin bar stops at 200. Each Condensed craft frees 60 (8 hours of regen), and every 60 you spend on a domain buys another 8. These are the moments you'd hit 200 if you did nothing.

Sources of Transient Resin

Transient Resin is stored as an item — it doesn't count against your 200 cap until used, so each one banks +60 resin on top of your stockpile. Every copy dies a fortnight after the Monday of the week you got it, whatever the source. Each bar is one copy in two halves: solid while you can still claim it, hollow for the days it's then yours before it expires. It assumes you claim as early as you can, so last week's copy still counts — it's in your bag until this Monday plus seven. Faded bars can't reach your target line.

Companionship EXP

This is the one that raises Friendship Level. Characters living in your Serenitea Pot generate it, and it stops dead at 500 — so bank it before a new character lands and pour it straight into them. Same hourly tick as Realm Currency.

5/hr is the ceiling, at 12,000 Adeptal Energy. Storage caps at 500 at Trust Rank 10, with room for 8 companions from Trust Rank 9.
